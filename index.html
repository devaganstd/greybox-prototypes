Greybox Prototypes

Early playable greyboxes. Grey shapes, core loop only.

Bounce FieldDon't aim, reflect. Rotate one mirror; every bounce raises damage. Shaft CollapseMerge miners, pull the prop, trigger a Cave-in Cascade. Tilt TrayNo timer. One swipe. Tilt the tray; every block slides at once. Lemonade ApocalypseServe lemonade to zombies; every cured customer can be hired as staff. Gray Town CaféGrey townsfolk get their colour back with one sip; every bright customer restores the town or joins your staff. Sweep the Planet (3D)Start with a toothbrush, clean a whole planet. Clean tiles light up and pay you, until the dust storm comes. Sweep the Planet (2D)Top-down version of the same loop.
